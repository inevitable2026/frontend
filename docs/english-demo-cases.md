# English demo cases

These are the two short change-detection moments to use in an English pitch.
They deliberately use jobsite vocabulary that is familiar without asking the
audience to know Korean construction terms such as `동바리`.

## 1. Concrete pour moved to night shift

**Korean cue**

작업 변경 감지: 4층 콘크리트 타설이 주간 작업에서 야간 작업으로 변경되었습니다.

**On-screen copy**

> Work change detected: The 4th-floor concrete pour was moved from the day shift to the night shift.

**Presenter line**

> The system detected that the 4th-floor concrete pour is now planned for the night shift, so it immediately asks the team to re-check lighting, visibility, and the night-work plan.

`Concrete pour` is the common site term here. Prefer `night shift` over just
`night`: it makes clear that the work schedule changed, not merely the time at
which an alert was viewed.

## 2. Slab formwork system changed

**Korean cue**

작업 변경 감지: 4층 슬래브 거푸집이 합판 거푸집에서 알루미늄 폼으로 변경되었습니다.

**On-screen copy**

> Work change detected: The 4th-floor slab formwork system changed from plywood forms to aluminum forms.

**Presenter line**

> The formwork system changed from plywood forms to aluminum forms. The system flags the change and brings up the safety review items that must be checked before work starts.

Use `forms` for the physical panels and `formwork system` for the installation
method as a whole. This reads more naturally to an English-speaking
construction audience than translating `동바리` directly as *slab shoring*.

## Demo sequencing

1. Show the concrete-pour schedule change and the resulting night-work checks.
2. Show the formwork-system change and the resulting safety-review checklist.
3. Close on the product behavior: it detects the field change, identifies the
   affected work, and prepares the next review instead of merely summarizing a
   document.
